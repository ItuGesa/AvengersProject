THOR

2011


Thor is to enherit the throne from his father Odin. On the day of his crowning, the enemies of the god's, the Frost Grants, enter the palace violating the treaty. His father Odin punishes himbu bansishing him to Earth and stripping him of his powers. Meanwhile his brother Loki has mischevious plans in Asgard. Thor faces greater threats without his power.
